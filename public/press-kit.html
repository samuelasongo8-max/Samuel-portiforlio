Press Kit: Samuel Asongo

Samuel Asongo is an artist and music educator based in Kakuma, Kenya. His work brings together creative practice, music learning, and community participation. He is also the founder of Art and Unity for Vulnerable Development (AUVD), an organization focused on arts, education, and community development.

Through his work, Samuel supports opportunities for people to learn, create, and share ideas. His interests include visual arts, music education, digital development, and community-centered collaboration. Media and partnership enquiries can use the contact details below. Please request permission before publishing photographs or stories involving students and community members.
